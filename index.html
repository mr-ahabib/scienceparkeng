About us

A leading laboratory equipment supplier in Bangladesh

Science Park Engineering is a leading provider, importer, and supplier of laboratory equipment in Bangladesh. We offer scientific instruments, chemicals, reagents, and consumables tailored to your sector — serving pharmaceuticals, education, research, textiles, and industrial quality control.

  • Laboratory instruments — chromatography, meters, glassware, and plasticware.
  • Textile testing, pharmaceutical tools, raw materials, and industrial QC products.
  • Kits for chemistry, biology, microbiology, water and environmental testing, and more.

Customer satisfaction drives our work. We aim to grow with our clients by meeting their needs with dependable products and responsive service.
